## How to Use This Repository

1. **Clone the repo** into your local environment.
2. **Run the verification script** (`word_count_check.py` in the `scratch/` folder) to confirm each article meets the required 1,000‑1,500 word range.
3. **Deploy** the Markdown files to your CMS (WordPress, Webflow, etc.) or keep them as a content repository for future blog creation.
4. **Update** any city‑specific details (e.g., new clinic locations) directly in `local_seo.md`.
5. **Track progress** using `task.md` – it reflects the current completion status.
