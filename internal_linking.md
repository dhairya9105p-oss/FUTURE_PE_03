# Internal Linking Strategy Matrix

An optimized internal linking structure helps distribute page authority (PageRank) across a website, improves navigation for patients, and helps search engines understand the semantic relationships between pages. Below is the detailed internal linking plan for our dental content pack.

---

## 1. General Internal Linking Rules

To maximize SEO efficiency, the clinic website should follow these guidelines:
1.  **Anchor Text Variety**: Avoid using generic anchor texts like "click here" or "read more". Use descriptive, keyword-rich anchor texts.
2.  **Top-Down and Bottom-Up Linking**: Every spoke page must link back to the primary pillar page (the homepage or location pillar page), and the pillar page must list and link to every spoke page.
3.  **Cross-Spoke Links**: Related spoke pages should link to each other (e.g., a dental implants guide linking to a root canal guide) to keep users engaged and guide them along the treatment funnel.
4.  **Natural Integration**: Links should only be placed where they make contextual sense to the reader, avoiding disruption of the reading flow.
